ISA Operational Framework

4-Phase Village Implementation Lifecycle

Structural lifecycle an ISA must complete per village under the JJM programme guidelines.

"Per SWSM-UP ISA Operational Guidelines, all village-level activities must complete Phase 1 community mobilization before any infrastructure works commence. No civil work sanction shall be issued without a constituted Paani Samiti (VWSC) and an approved Village Action Plan (VAP)." — SWSM UP, ISA Framework Guidelines, 2021
1
Phase 1 · Mobilization

Fitment & Initial Community Mobilization

  • Village-level fitment surveys & household enumeration
  • Jan Andolan awareness drives in target gram panchayats
  • Nukkad Nataks (street plays) on safe water & sanitation
  • Coordination with Block Development Officers (BDOs)
  • Baseline data collection for FHTC demand mapping
2
Phase 2 · Governance

Paani Samiti Constitution (VWSC)

  • Formation of Village Water & Sanitation Committee (VWSC)
  • Minimum 50% women representation mandate enforced
  • Elections of Adhyaksha, Sachiv & Kosh Prabhandak
  • Bank account opening & formal registration with Gram Sabha
  • Capacity building for Paani Samiti members on governance
3
Phase 3 · Planning

Village Action Plan (VAP) Engineering

  • Water demand computation based on household surveys
  • Source water quality testing & chemical analysis
  • Greywater & liquid waste management planning
  • DPR finalization with DPMU & technical sanction
  • Geo-tagging of assets and infrastructure mapping
4
Phase 4 · Handover & Sustainability

Community Handover & FTK Skilling

  • Infrastructure commissioning & completion certificate
  • Training 5 women per village via Field Test Kits (FTK)
  • Water quality monitoring protocols handover to VWSC
  • Operation & Maintenance (O&M) budget planning
  • Final Paani Samiti empowerment certification
